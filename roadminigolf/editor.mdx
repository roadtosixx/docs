# Course Editor

Admins build courses directly in the world: up to 36 holes per course, anywhere
on the map, from the bundled [track kit](/roadminigolf/track-kit) or from any
game prop.

Open the editor with **F7** or `/mgedit`. Only [admins](/roadminigolf/configuration#permissions)
can open it.

## The two modes

| Mode | What you do there | Switch |
|---|---|---|
| **Panel** | Course list, course settings, holes, props, admin tools | **Tab** opens the panel |
| **World** | Place, select, move and turn props with the crosshair or the mouse cursor | **Tab** or **Esc** goes back to the world |

The panel opens on the course list, with the tabs **Courses** and
[**Admin**](#admin-console). An open course has the tabs **General** (name,
description, start point, blip, publishing), **Holes** (holes, tee, cup, par,
play area, build tools, course check), **Props** (catalog and placed props) and
**Settings** (rules and money of this course).

The top of the panel holds a **world toolbar** with the camera mode and the
most used tools (world mode, kit pieces, quick build, draw path, camera, test
the open hole), so most work needs no Tab.

## Building faster

### Quick build (1)

A whole hole in the world, without the panel.

1. A tee ghost follows the pointer. **Wheel** or **← →** turns it, **LMB** /
   **Enter** sets it. This creates a new hole with its tee.
2. The next piece waits on the open lane end. **← →** (or the wheel) steps
   through the pieces, **↑ ↓** picks another open end when there are several.
   **LMB** / **Enter** places the piece, **Backspace** removes the newest one,
   **B** recolours the whole hole.
3. A cup piece finishes the hole: tee and cup are set, and the par and play area
   are suggested from the lane. **3** tests the hole right away; afterwards the
   editor comes back in world mode with the same camera.
4. **RMB** / **Esc** leaves quick build, **Tab** opens the panel. Everything
   placed stays.

A bar at the bottom right shows the piece, the pieces so far, the lane length
and the suggested par.

### Draw path (2)

Click the lane on the ground, and the hole is built from kit pieces.

- **LMB** adds a point (the first one is the tee), **Backspace** removes the
  last one, **Enter** places the hole. The finished hole shows as ghosts while
  you draw.
- The lane runs in 45° steps. Straights come from 4, 2 and 1 m pieces, corners
  from 45° and 90° curves, and a height step of 0.4 m or more between two
  points becomes ramps when the run is long enough.
- **Wheel** / **← →** turns the tee away from the automatic direction, **R**
  goes back to automatic, **B** picks the colour.
- The bar warns when a corner is too tight or a ramp does not fit, and shows
  how far the cup is off the last click.

### Blueprint

A 2D hole builder inside the panel: tab **Holes** → **Blueprint**.

- **Click** a piece in the palette to continue the lane at the open end, or
  **drag** it onto the grid; it snaps to the nearest open lane end.
- **Drag** a piece to move it, **drag** the background to pan, **Wheel** zooms,
  **F** fits the hole. **R** turns a piece, **Del** removes it,
  **Ctrl + Z / Y** undo and redo inside the blueprint, **Esc** closes it.
- Open lane ends show as white arrows; ramps show their rise and every raised
  piece its height. The side panel has the label, colour theme, par, lane
  length and the check (one tee, a cup, no open lane end, no overlapping
  pieces).
- **Place in the world** turns the blueprint into a new hole that follows the
  crosshair. **Edit in blueprint** on an existing hole opens its track pieces,
  and **Apply to hole** rebuilds it in place in one undo step.
- **Save as own blueprint** keeps it on your client (24 slots) next to the
  prebuilt holes, where you can place, open, rename or delete it.

<Tip>
  Quick build, drawn paths, blueprints and prebuilt holes get a **suggested par
  and play area** automatically. Both stay editable in the Holes tab. See
  [Track kit](/roadminigolf/track-kit#suggested-par-and-play-area) for how they
  are worked out.
</Tip>

## Moving things

### Mouse cursor and cameras

- **Mouse cursor (4)**: point at props and click instead of aiming the
  crosshair. On by default and remembered per admin. Hold **RMB** to look
  around; a short right click cancels a placement.
- **Camera modes (5)**: *orbit* circles the selection or the hole
  (**RMB + mouse** orbits, **Q/E** zooms, **WASD** moves the focus),
  *top-down* looks straight down on the whole hole, *free* is the fly camera.
- **Focus (6)** flies to the selection and frames it.
- **Freecam (F)**: **WASD** moves, **Q/E** down and up, **Shift** fast,
  **Alt** slow. **H** moves your player to the camera.

### Gizmo

A selected prop or group shows three arrows (X red, Y green, Z blue) and a
yellow ring. Drag an arrow with **LMB** to slide along it, or the ring to turn
it. **Shift** makes it fine, the grid (**V**) snaps it. Every drag is one undo
step; kit pieces never tilt, and their tees and cups move along.

### Groups

**Ctrl + click** (in the world or in the prop list) adds props to a group. The
group turns around its centre, lifts, moves (**G**), duplicates (**C**) and
deletes as one; kit tees and cups take their holes along.

### Whole holes and courses

- **Copy, paste and duplicate holes** with all their props, also into other
  courses.
- **Duplicate a course** from the course list.
- **Move course here** moves the whole course so its start point sits where you
  stand, turned to your facing direction. It can be undone.

## Testing

**Test** on a hole (or **3** in the world) plays it right away with the real
ball physics:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **R** | Put the ball back on the tee |
| **Enter** | After holing: next hole |
| **Backspace** | Back to the editor |

## Course check and saving

The **course check** in the Holes tab lists holes without tee or cup, cups
outside the play area, open lane ends of the track kit, a par above the stroke
limit and stray props. It is summarised before saving and never blocks a save.

<Warning>
  A save that changes the **number of holes or the total par** asks first,
  because it starts a new leaderboard for that course. The old board is
  archived.
</Warning>

- **Undo / redo**: **Ctrl + Z** / **Ctrl + Y** for every change.
- **Drafts**: unsaved work survives crashes and restarts. Every working copy
  keeps its own draft, and only you delete it (**Discard**).
- **Two admins on one course**: saving detects when another admin saved the
  same course in the meantime and asks before overwriting their changes.
- **Running rounds are safe**: saving never pulls the props from under a
  running round. Its players keep the layout they are scored on; the new one
  appears after the round.

## Publishing

New courses are **unpublished** (unless `Config.Editor.publishNewCourses` is
on). Players cannot start unpublished courses; admins can still play them solo
to test. Publish in the **General** tab or with the button in the course list.

## Course settings

Every course can override the server defaults in its **Settings** tab:

| Setting | Server default |
|---|---|
| Max. players | 8 |
| Max. strokes per hole | `Config.Game.maxStrokes` |
| Time per hole | `Config.Game.holeTimeLimit` |
| Out-of-bounds penalty | `Config.Game.outOfBoundsPenalty` |
| Out of bounds: play area or lane only | `Config.Game.defaultOobMode` |
| Entry fee, finish reward, win reward | `Config.Economy` |
| Aim guide with roll preview | On |
| Teleport players to each tee | `Config.Game.teleportToTee` |
| Flags at the cups | On |

## Admin console

The **Admin** tab of the course list:

- **Live games and lobbies**: end any session; paid entry fees are refunded.
- **Leaderboards**: remove single entries or reset a course's board.
- **History**: restore an older saved version of a course
  (`Config.Editor.historySize`, 10 per course). It is saved as a new version.
- **Deleted courses**: restore a deleted course from the trash
  (`Config.Editor.trashSize`, 20).

## Export and import

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/mgexport <courseId>` | Writes the course to `data/export/<courseId>.json` |
| `/mgimport <file.json>` | Imports `data/import/<file.json>` as a new course with a new ID |

`/mgcourses` lists every course with its ID.

## All editor controls

The tool keys **1–6** are FiveM key mappings. If another resource uses the same
key, rebind them under *Settings → Key Bindings → FiveM* ("Minigolf editor: …");
the hint bar always shows the bound key. Server defaults:
`Config.Editor.keys`.

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Open / close | **F7** or `/mgedit` |
| Panel / world | **Tab** (panel) / **Tab** or **Esc** (world) |
| Freecam | **F**; **WASD**, **Q/E** down/up, **Shift** fast, **Alt** slow |
| Quick build | **1** |
| Draw path | **2** |
| Test the hole | **3** |
| Mouse cursor on / off | **4** (hold **RMB** to look around) |
| Camera mode: orbit → top-down → free | **5** |
| Fly to the selection | **6** |
| Player to camera | **H** |
| Select a prop | **LMB** |
| Move / duplicate / delete the selection | **G** / **C** / **Del** |
| Gizmo: slide / turn | Drag an arrow / the ring with **LMB** (**Shift** fine) |
| Rotate | **Wheel** or **← →** (**Shift** fine, **Ctrl** coarse) |
| Tilt / roll | **↑ ↓** / **Alt + ↑ ↓** |
| Height | **PgUp** / **PgDn** |
| Reset rotation and height | **R** |
| Align to the surface (placing) | **G** |
| Snap to the nearest prop (placing) | **X**, the wheel then turns in 90° steps |
| Grid for position and heading | **V** |
| Add to / remove from the group | **Ctrl + LMB** (world) or **Ctrl + click** (prop list) |
| Track kit: connect to an open lane end | Automatic, **X** toggles; the wheel picks the connector |
| Track kit: colour theme | **B** (**Shift + B** backwards) |
| Place / place and keep going / cancel | **LMB** or **Enter** / **Shift + LMB** / **RMB** or **Backspace** |
| Undo / redo | **Ctrl + Z** / **Ctrl + Y** |
