# Playing

## Finding a course

Every published course has a start point with a marker (or an ox_target /
qb-target zone) and a blip on the map.

- **`/minigolf`** opens the course finder: every open course, nearest first,
  with holes, par, open lobbies, the course record, your own best and a
  waypoint button.
- At the start point, **E** opens the course menu. `/mgmenu` opens the menu of
  the nearest course start, or your lobby.

<Info>
  To open, join or start a game you have to stand at the course start
  (`Config.Game.startDistance`, 50 m by default) and be out of your vehicle.
  Server owners can block players who are dead, cuffed or jailed through
  [hooks](/roadminigolf/configuration#hooks).
</Info>

## The course menu

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play solo** | Starts a round on your own. It counts for the leaderboard. |
| **Create lobby** | Opens a lobby you host. Other players join it from the same menu. |
| **Open lobbies** | Lobbies on this course you can join. |
| **Practice** | Play on your own from any hole: no score, no money, nothing sent to the server. Server owners can turn it off (`Config.Game.practiceMode`). |
| **Leaderboard** | All time, this month and this week, plus the record of every hole. |

The menu also shows the entry fee and rewards (if the course has any), the time
limit per hole and your own statistics.

## Lobbies

The host starts the game and can remove players. A lobby holds up to 8
players, and every player gets their own colour.

### Inviting players

- **Invite nearby players** from the lobby, or invite anyone with
  `/mginvite <server id>`.
- The invited player sees a prompt and accepts with **Y** or declines with
  **Backspace**, no menu needed. Players who are too far away get a waypoint
  to the course.
- Players near the course start are told when a new lobby opens there
  (`Config.Game.announceLobbies`).
- Lobby members who walk away from the course leave the lobby automatically
  (`Config.Game.lobbyLeaveDistance`, 60 m).

### Game modes

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Everyone at once" icon="users">
    Everybody plays their own ball at the same time. The fastest flow, and the
    default.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Take turns" icon="repeat">
    Classic rules: one player plays the hole out while the others watch. The
    best score on a hole starts the next one.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

In **Take turns**, nobody waits forever: an idle player sees a countdown and is
moved on after `Config.Game.turnIdleTimeout` (90 s), and the host can skip an
idle turn after 30 seconds.

### Lobby rules

The host can change the **stroke limit**, the **time per hole** and the **aim
guide**, and can make the lobby **private** (invite only). A lobby with changed
rules is marked **unranked** and stays off the leaderboard.

<Note>
  The range the host may pick from is set in `Config.Game.lobbyRules`
  (3 to 15 strokes, 0 to 600 seconds per hole by default).
</Note>

## Playing a hole

<Steps>
  <Step title="Hole intro">
    A short camera flight from the cup to the tee with a hole card: par,
    length and the hole record. Skip it with **E**, **Space** or **Backspace**,
    or turn it off in your settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Address the ball">
    Walk to your ball and press **E**. The player steps up beside the ball with
    the putter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Aim">
    Aim with the **mouse**, fine-tune with **← →**, zoom with the **wheel**.
    **C** shows an overview of the whole hole. The **aim guide** simulates the
    real roll while you charge: slopes, ramps and walls bend the line, and it
    ends in the cup when the putt drops. Courses can turn it off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shoot">
    Hold **LMB** or **E** to charge and release to hit. **RMB** or
    **Backspace** cancels a charged shot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Power control

Each player picks one in their settings. Server default:
`Config.Game.defaultPowerMode`.

| Mode | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Timing** (`'timing'`) | Hold to swing back, release at the right moment. The meter rises and falls. |
| **Precision** (`'drag'`) | Hold, pull the mouse back to set the power, release to hit. Right click cancels. |

### Rules and penalties

| Rule | Default | Config key |
|---|---|---|
| Stroke limit per hole | 10 | `Config.Game.maxStrokes` |
| Time limit per hole | 180 s (0 = off), with countdown beeps in the last 10 s | `Config.Game.holeTimeLimit` |
| Out of bounds | +1 stroke | `Config.Game.outOfBoundsPenalty` |
| Put ball back (unplayable lie, from the game menu) | +1 stroke | `Config.Game.resetPenalty` |

Each course can override the stroke limit, time limit and penalty in the
editor.

**Out of bounds** works in one of two ways, set per course:

- **Play area**: the ball is out when it leaves the hole's play area (a radius
  around the hole).
- **Lane only**: the ball is out as soon as it comes to rest on ground that is
  not part of the course. The edge of the play area shows up when the ball gets
  close.

### Shot feedback

Lip-outs, near misses, a last-stroke warning, a camera punch on full-power hits
and a trail behind rolling balls in the player colour. Holes-in-one get a
celebration, and the other players are told.

## Track kit obstacles

- **Windmill**: the sails block the tunnel in time with the network clock, so
  every player sees them in the same place. A sail pushes a ball out of its
  way.
- **Loop**: the ball only makes it round with enough speed and comes out in the
  channel beside the one it went in. Too slow and it rolls back or drops off.

## During the round

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **Z** (hold) | Scoreboard |
| **F9** | Game menu: put the ball back, settings, leave |
| **← →** | After finishing: spectate the others (stop with **Backspace**) |

Players near a running game see its balls and the putters of the players
(`Config.World.showBystanderBalls`, needs OneSync).

## Results and leaderboards

At the end, the results show your place, the course record, your personal best
and the gap to your previous best, whether the round was ranked, and why a
reward was not paid (if it wasn't). **Play again** offers a rematch to the whole
group; the offer stays as a prompt after closing the results.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="When does a round count for the leaderboard?">
    Solo rounds and lobbies with the course's own rules count. Lobbies with
    changed rules are unranked. Rounds flagged by the anti-cheat checks are
    kept off the leaderboard by default (`Config.AntiCheat.skipLeaderboardOnFlag`).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did the leaderboard of a course start over?">
    When an admin changes the number of playable holes or the total par of a
    course, its leaderboard and hole records start over. The old board is
    archived, and rounds that were running on the old layout do not enter the
    new one.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When are rewards paid?">
    Only when the server owner set rewards, and only for rounds where every hole
    was holed. The win reward goes to a sole winner only (ties get none). A
    cooldown per player and course and a minimum round duration apply. Entry
    fees are refunded whenever a game is cancelled.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Player settings

Open the game menu (**F9**) → **Settings**. Settings are stored per player.

| Setting | What it changes |
|---|---|
| Power control | Timing or Precision |
| Aim sensitivity / Pull sensitivity | How fast aiming and pulling back react |
| Ball camera | The camera follows your ball after the shot |
| Names above other balls | Player names over other players' balls |
| Hole intro | Camera flight over each new hole and its hole card |
| Ball trail | A short trail behind rolling balls |
| Ball | Your golf ball design, used from your next game |

## All player controls

All keys are FiveM key mappings. Players can change them under
*Settings → Key Bindings → FiveM*.

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Open the course menu at the start point | **E** (or ox_target / qb-target) |
| Address the ball | **E** near your ball |
| Aim | **Mouse**, fine with **← →**, zoom with the **wheel** |
| Overview of the hole | **C** |
| Shoot (Timing) | Hold **LMB** or **E**, release |
| Shoot (Precision) | Hold **LMB** or **E**, pull the mouse back, release |
| Cancel a charged shot / step back | **RMB** or **Backspace** |
| Scoreboard | Hold **Z** |
| Game menu | **F9** |
| Spectate after finishing / watch in Take turns | **← →**, stop with **Backspace** |
| Skip the hole intro | **E**, **Space** or **Backspace** |
| Accept / decline an invite or rematch | **Y** / **Backspace** |
| Leave | Game menu or `/mgleave` |
