# Track Kit

Road Minigolf streams its own modular track kit: felt lanes with rounded walls
and real cup holes, obstacles and two moving pieces. Every piece takes one of
**16 colour themes**. In the editor you find it in tab **Props** → category
**Track kit**.

## Pieces

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Lanes">
    | Piece | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | Lane 1 m, 2 m, 4 m | Straight lanes |
    | Curve 45°, Curve 90° | Turn left or right, picked with the wheel |
    | Ramp 0.5 m | Up onto a raised section, or down |
    | Hill | A bump in the lane |
    | Waves | Several bumps in a row |
    | Narrow | The lane gets tighter |
    | Island split | The lane splits around an island |
    | Dead end | Closes a lane |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Tee & cup">
    | Piece | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | Tee | Tee mat set into the felt. Placing it sets the hole's tee. |
    | Cup (end) | Cup at the end of the lane. Placing it sets the hole's cup. |
    | Cup (lane) | Cup inside a lane the ball can roll past. |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Obstacles">
    | Piece | Notes |
    |---|---|
    | Windmill | The sails turn in front of the house and block the tunnel through it |
    | Tunnel | A roofed section of lane |
    | Loop | A spiral loop the ball rides only with enough speed |
    | Bumper post, Block, Deflector, Pyramid | Free-standing obstacles to put on a lane |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Moving pieces

- **Windmill**: the sails turn in time with the network clock, so every player
  sees them in the same place. A sail pushes a ball out of its way.
- **Loop**: the ball enters in one channel and comes out in the channel beside
  it. Too slow and it rolls back or drops off. Players cannot walk through the
  loop.

## Colour themes

Fairway, Ocean, Cherry, Grape, Tangerine, Lagoon, Bubblegum, Sunshine, Stone,
Midnight, Sand, Mint, Coral, Royal, Forest and Arctic.

**B** cycles the theme of the piece you are placing or the selected piece
(**Shift + B** backwards). In quick build, **B** recolours the whole hole.

## Building with the kit

- Pieces **lock onto open lane ends** by themselves. The wheel picks the
  connector (turn left or right, flip a piece); **X** turns locking off and on.
- Placing a **Tee** or a **Cup** piece sets the hole's tee or cup.
- Kit pieces stay level: only the heading turns.
- The **course check** lists open lane ends where the ball could roll off.

## Prebuilt holes

Six complete holes are ready to place: tab **Holes** → **Prebuilt holes** →
**Place**, then put it down with the crosshair. Tee, cup and par are set.

| Hole | Par | Features |
|---|---|---|
| Windmill | 3 | Curve onto a windmill |
| Ramp | 3 | Ramp onto a raised section, curve and hill |
| Loop | 3 | Loop, island split, narrow lane and waves |
| Tunnel | 3 | Post, block and pyramid in front of a tunnel |
| Zigzag | 3 | 45° and 90° curves with a deflector and waves |
| Hills | 2 | Hills and waves |

The prebuilt holes also open in the [blueprint](/roadminigolf/editor#blueprint)
as a starting point for your own.

## Suggested par and play area

Quick build, drawn paths, blueprints and prebuilt holes without their own
values get a suggested par and play area. Both stay editable in the Holes tab.

**Par**: 2, plus one stroke for every full 6 m of lane beyond the first 4 m,
plus one for every two of loop, windmill, tunnel and uphill ramp, at most 6.

**Play area**: the farthest piece from the hole centre plus 4 m, at least 8 m.

<Accordion title="Change the formula">
  The values live in `shared/defaults.lua` and can be added to `Config.Editor`
  in `config.lua`:

  ```lua config.lua
  Config.Editor.autoParBase = 2         -- par of a short hole
  Config.Editor.autoParFreeLength = 4.0 -- m of lane before length adds strokes
  Config.Editor.autoParPerLength = 6.0  -- m of lane per extra stroke
  Config.Editor.autoParMax = 6
  Config.Editor.boundsMargin = 4.0      -- m around the farthest piece
  Config.Editor.boundsMin = 8.0
  ```

  Add these lines **after** the `Config.Editor = { … }` table, or put the keys
  inside it.
</Accordion>

## Other props

Besides the kit, the editor has a catalog of base-game and stunt DLC props in
four categories: **Lanes & ramps**, **Borders & walls**, **Obstacles** and
**Decoration**. With `Config.Editor.allowCustomModels = true` admins can also
type any model name.

- Models a game build does not have are hidden automatically.
- `Config.Editor.blockedModels` blocks model names you do not want on courses.
- `Config.PropCatalog` in `config.lua` is the catalog itself: add, remove or
  rename entries and categories.

```lua config.lua
Config.PropCatalog = {
    {
        label = 'Obstacles',
        items = {
            { model = 'prop_mp_cone_01', label = 'Cone red' },
            { model = 'prop_haybale_01', label = 'Hay bale 1' },
        },
    },
}
```

<Note>
  The ball rolls on every prop with the same raycast physics: slopes, ramps,
  walls and drops all work. **X** snaps a prop flush against its nearest neighbour, which
  makes lanes from stunt tracks or barriers piece by piece much easier.
</Note>
