# Exports

Call the exports on `exports.road_banking`. Unless noted, they are
server-side.

## Targets

Most exports take a `target`. It can be:

| Value | Account |
|---|---|
| Player server id (number) | That player's checking account |
| Account number (string, with or without spaces) | That account |
| Job name with a business account (string) | The job's business account |
| Framework identifier (string) | That player's checking account, if it exists |

Amounts are whole numbers above zero.

## Accounts

### GetPersonalAccount

<ParamField path="target" type="number | string" required>
  See [Targets](#targets)
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="string | nil">
  The account number of the checking account, or `nil`.
</ResponseField>

```lua
local number = exports.road_banking:GetPersonalAccount(source) -- 'LS1234567890'
```

### GetAccount

<ParamField path="target" type="number | string" required>
  See [Targets](#targets)
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="table | nil">
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ResponseField name="number" type="string">Account number</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="type" type="string">`personal`, `savings`, `shared` or `business`</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="label" type="string">Display name</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="owner" type="string">Identifier of the owner, or the job name</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="balance" type="number | nil">`nil` for a checking account whose owner is offline</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="frozen" type="boolean">Frozen by an admin</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

```lua
local account = exports.road_banking:GetAccount('police')
print(account.label, account.balance)
```

### GetBalance

<ParamField path="target" type="number | string" required>
  See [Targets](#targets)
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="number">
  The balance. `0` when the account does not exist or is a checking account
  whose owner is offline.
</ResponseField>

## Money

### AddMoney

Pays money into an account and adds a history line.

<ParamField path="target" type="number | string" required>
  See [Targets](#targets)
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="amount" type="number" required>
  Whole amount above zero
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="reason" type="string">
  Shown in the history (up to 120 characters)
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="boolean">
  `true` when the money was booked.
</ResponseField>

```lua
exports.road_banking:AddMoney(source, 2500, 'Paycheck')
```

### RemoveMoney

Takes money from an account and adds a history line. Refuses overdrafts and
frozen accounts.

<ParamField path="target" type="number | string" required>
  See [Targets](#targets)
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="amount" type="number" required>
  Whole amount above zero
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="reason" type="string">
  Shown in the history (up to 120 characters)
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="boolean">
  `true` when the money was taken.
</ResponseField>

```lua
if exports.road_banking:RemoveMoney(source, 800, 'Vehicle repair') then
    -- repair the vehicle
end
```

### Transfer

Moves money between two accounts, with history lines on both sides. The
recipient is notified. `Config.Transfers` fees do not apply; pass your own
with `options.fee`.

<ParamField path="from" type="number | string" required>
  Paying account, see [Targets](#targets)
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="to" type="number | string" required>
  Receiving account
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="amount" type="number" required>
  Whole amount above zero
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="reference" type="string">
  Shown in both histories (up to 120 characters)
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="options" type="table">
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ParamField path="fee" type="number">Charged to the payer on top of the amount and booked as a bank fee</ParamField>
    <ParamField path="notify" type="boolean" default="true">`false` sends no RoadBanking notification to the recipient, so your script can show its own</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="boolean, string | nil">
  `true` on success, otherwise `false` and an error code such as
  `insufficient_funds`, `account_frozen`, `owner_offline`, `invalid_amount`
  or `invalid_request`.
</ResponseField>

When `from` is a player server id, that player is recorded as the one who
made the transfer.

```lua
local ok, code = exports.road_banking:Transfer(source, 'mechanic', 1500, 'Tuning')
local ok2 = exports.road_banking:Transfer(source, 'LS1234567890', 950, 'Rent', { fee = 50, notify = false })
```

### AddTransaction

Adds a history line for money your script moved itself, for example through
the framework's own bank functions. The balance does not change.

<ParamField path="target" type="number | string" required>
  See [Targets](#targets)
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="amount" type="number" required>
  Positive for incoming, negative for outgoing money
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="reference" type="string">
  Up to 120 characters
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="counterpartyName" type="string">
  Who paid or was paid (up to 64 characters)
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="boolean" />

```lua
exports.road_banking:AddTransaction(source, -45, 'Fuel', 'Globe Oil')
```

### GetTransactions

<ParamField path="target" type="number | string" required>
  See [Targets](#targets)
</ParamField>
<ParamField path="limit" type="number" default="20">
  Up to 100
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="table">
  Newest first.

  <Expandable title="entry">
    <ResponseField name="id" type="number">Ledger id</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="kind" type="string">For example `deposit`, `withdraw`, `transfer_in`, `transfer_out`, `fee`, `interest`, `external`</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="label" type="string">Readable text for the kind in the server language (from `public/locales`), e.g. "Cash deposit"</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amount" type="number">Negative for outgoing money</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="counterparty" type="string">Other account number, if any</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="counterpartyName" type="string">Other party's name, if any</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reference" type="string">Reference text</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="createdAt" type="number">Timestamp in milliseconds</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Invoices

### CreateInvoice

Sends an invoice to an online player, for fines, shops or services. The
recipient pays it in the bank.

<ParamField path="data" type="table" required>
  <Expandable title="properties">
    <ParamField path="target" type="number" required>Server id of the player who pays</ParamField>
    <ParamField path="amount" type="number" required>Whole amount above zero, up to `Config.Invoices.MaxAmount`</ParamField>
    <ParamField path="reason" type="string">Shown on the invoice (up to 120 characters)</ParamField>
    <ParamField path="job" type="string">Business account that receives the money</ParamField>
    <ParamField path="sender" type="number">Server id of the sending player; the money goes to their checking account. Give `job` or `sender`.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="boolean, number | string">
  `true` and the invoice id, or `false` and an error code.
</ResponseField>

```lua
exports.road_banking:CreateInvoice({ target = playerId, amount = 500, reason = 'Speeding', job = 'police' })
```

## Credit score

### GetCreditScore

<ParamField path="target" type="number | string" required>
  Player server id or framework identifier
</ParamField>

<ResponseField name="return" type="number | nil">
  The score from 300 to 850, without the points for money held, which are
  only counted at the bank.
</ResponseField>

## Client

### IsOpen

<ResponseField name="return" type="boolean">
  `true` while the bank or ATM screen is open.
</ResponseField>

```lua
if exports.road_banking:IsOpen() then return end
```
