# Commands

## /bankadmin

Look up, freeze and correct any account. Needs the `road_banking.admin`
permission (`Config.AdminAce`) and also works from the server console.

```cfg server.cfg
add_ace group.admin road_banking.admin allow
```

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/bankadmin info <account number>` | Type, owner, balance and whether the account is frozen |
| `/bankadmin freeze <account number>` | Freezes the account: RoadBanking moves no money out of it |
| `/bankadmin unfreeze <account number>` | Lifts the freeze |
| `/bankadmin give <account number> <amount>` | Adds money, booked as an admin line in the history |
| `/bankadmin take <account number> <amount>` | Removes money; fails when the balance is too low |

<ParamField path="account number" type="string" required>
  Without spaces, for example `LS1234567890`. Players see their numbers in the
  bank.
</ParamField>

<Note>
  A checking account is the player's framework bank money. A freeze stops
  RoadBanking from taking money out, but other scripts that charge the
  framework balance directly are not affected. `take` on a checking account
  only works while its owner is online.
</Note>

<ParamField path="amount" type="number">
  Whole amount for `give` and `take`.
</ParamField>

Freezes and money corrections are posted to the
[Discord audit log](/roadbanking/configuration#audit-log) when one is set.
