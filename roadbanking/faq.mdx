# FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Players see two options at ATMs or bank counters">
    Another banking script also adds ATM and counter interactions. Stop it or
    remove its targets. Keep the resource that holds your society money
    running if your business accounts use it (`Config.Business.Source =
    'society'`).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Players can only have one card">
    Your inventory has no item metadata (for example the default ESX
    inventory), or `Config.Cards.Item = false`. Cards are then bound to the
    player, one per player. With ox_inventory, qb-inventory and other metadata
    inventories, cards are items and players can hold several. See
    [Cards and ATMs](/roadbanking/cards).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The card item has no picture">
    Copy `install/bank_card.png` into your inventory's image folder
    (`ox_inventory/web/images/` or `qb-inventory/html/images/`) and restart the
    inventory.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A card says it is blocked">
    The PIN was entered wrong too often. The card owner sets a new PIN in the
    **Cards** tab at a branch, which unblocks it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="There is no Deposit or Withdraw button">
    On a standalone server there is no cash, so deposits and withdrawals are
    hidden. Transfers, cards, loans, invoices and standing orders work.

    At an ATM, deposits are hidden when `Config.ATM.Deposit = false`. For
    savings accounts, cash withdrawals at the counter need
    `Config.Savings.CashWithdraw = true`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A loan installment or standing order was not taken">
    The checking account is the player's framework bank money and can only be
    charged while the player is online. Installments and standing orders from
    it wait until the player is back.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A business account does not show up">
    The job must be listed in `Config.Business.Accounts`, and the player's
    grade must be at least the `view` grade. The boss grade always has access.
    Players only see the business account of their current job.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The teller stands in the wrong place">
    The teller stands `Config.Teller.Offset` metres in front of the branch
    point, in the direction of its heading. Change the offset, or the heading
    (fourth value of `coords`) so it points from the player to the counter.
    `Config.Teller.Enabled = false` removes tellers.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add or move a branch?">
    Stand where players should stand at the counter, facing it, and add a line
    to `Config.Branches` with your coordinates and heading. See
    [Branches](/roadbanking/configuration#branches).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it work with RoadPhone Pro?">
    Yes, with RoadPhone Pro versions that have `Config.roadBanking`. Set
    `Config.roadBanking = true` in RoadPhone's `config.lua`. The phone's bank
    app then shows the RoadBanking checking account number, balance and
    history, and phone transfers go through RoadBanking: to any account number,
    or by phone number to a contact's checking account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can other scripts pay into or charge accounts?">
    Yes. Use the [server exports](/roadbanking/api/exports): `AddMoney`,
    `RemoveMoney`, `Transfer` and `CreateInvoice`. Scripts that change the
    framework bank balance directly keep working too; with `AddTransaction`
    they can add a matching line to the history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The bank does not open">
    Check the server console for `[road_banking]` errors. `oxmysql` and
    `ox_lib` must start before RoadBanking, and OneSync must be on. Players
    also cannot open the bank while dead, cuffed or in a vehicle; you can
    change that in `client/open.lua`.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
