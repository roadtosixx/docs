# Installation

RoadPark ships as 17 resources: the park map, the ticket booth, the entrance
sign, six rides, the games, the food stands and the seasonal
[Halloween decoration](/roadpark/halloween). Install them together — the map
places every attraction into its slot as soon as that resource starts.

<Danger>
  **Do not use FileZilla**. It can corrupt or skip hidden files during upload.
  We recommend **WinSCP**.
</Danger>

<Note>
  **There is no SQL file and no transaction ID to enter.** RoadPark keeps its
  data in JSON files and server KVP. The download comes from the
  [cfx.re Portal](/license-system).
</Note>

## 🚀 Setup Instructions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Extract the resources">
    Put all 17 folders into one category folder. Keep the folder names exactly
    as they are — the map, the ticket booth and the rides find each other by
    resource name.

    ```bash
    resources/
    └── [roadpark]/
        ├── roadpark/
        ├── roadpark_tickets/
        ├── roadpark_entrance_sign/
        ├── roadpark_breakdance/
        ├── roadpark_highlander/
        ├── roadpark_autoscooter/
        ├── roadpark_riesenrad/
        ├── roadpark_swingboat/
        ├── roadpark_wellenflug/
        ├── roadpark_games/
        ├── roadpark_ducks/
        ├── roadpark_claw/
        ├── roadpark_pizza/
        ├── roadpark_icecream/
        ├── roadpark_sweets/
        ├── roadpark_drinks/
        └── roadpark_halloween/
    ```
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the resources">
    Start your framework and your inventory first, then RoadPark:

    ```cfg server.cfg
    ensure roadpark
    ensure roadpark_tickets
    ensure roadpark_games
    ensure roadpark_breakdance
    ensure roadpark_highlander
    ensure roadpark_autoscooter
    ensure roadpark_riesenrad
    ensure roadpark_swingboat
    ensure roadpark_wellenflug
    ensure roadpark_pizza
    ensure roadpark_icecream
    ensure roadpark_sweets
    ensure roadpark_drinks
    ensure roadpark_ducks
    ensure roadpark_claw
    ensure roadpark_entrance_sign
    ensure roadpark_halloween
    ```

    The order inside this block does not decide placement: a ride that starts
    after the map still receives its slot, and every ride registers its price
    with the ticket booth again when `roadpark_tickets` (re)starts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give your staff permissions">
    ```cfg server.cfg
    add_ace group.admin roadpark.editor allow
    add_ace group.admin roadpark.admin allow
    ```

    `roadpark.editor` unlocks the placement editors of the booths, games and
    most rides. `roadpark.admin` unlocks the Highlander editor. Players need no
    permission to ride, play or buy. Details: [Permissions](/roadpark/configuration#permissions).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Register the inventory items">
    Park Chips and the prizes are real items. Add them to your inventory — see
    [Inventory items](#inventory-items) below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Join and check">
    Connect, follow the **RoadPark** blip to the entrance at Mirror Park and buy
    a chip at the ticket booth. The price board on the booth lists every ride
    that is running.

    Each resource with a bridge prints the framework and inventory it found in
    the server console, for example `[Bridge] ESX + ox_inventory`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## ⚠️ Map compatibility

The `roadpark` resource replaces the original game files of the Mirror Park
construction site and a few neighbouring LOD, road and collision files while
it runs. Your GTA V installation is never modified.

<Warning>
  Do not run another map that streams files with the same names — for example
  a different build on the Mirror Park construction site. Two maps replacing
  the same files overwrite each other, and you get holes or floating objects.
</Warning>

While the park runs, the roads inside the park area are closed for ambient
traffic and the parked-car spawns there are removed. Both come back when you
stop `roadpark`.

## 🔄 Updating

<Steps>
  <Step title="Keep your server-owned files">
    Copy the new files over the old ones, but keep:

    - every `config.lua` you changed (and `roadpark_games/config/*.lua`)
    - `placement.json`, `managed_placement.json` and their backups in the
      ride and booth folders
    - `roadpark_games/scores.json` (the leaderboards)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart">
    `refresh`, then `restart` the resources you updated. Copying files alone
    does not restart anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconnect">
    After an update with new streamed files (models, map, collision),
    reconnect your client so the new files load instead of the cached ones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## 🗑️ Removing the park

Remove the `ensure` lines of `roadpark` and of every attraction you do not use
elsewhere, restart the server and reconnect. The original construction site,
its roads and its parked cars come back.

Attractions can also live on without the park — see
[Placing attractions outside the park](/roadpark/configuration#placing-attractions-outside-the-park).
