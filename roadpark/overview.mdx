# RoadPark

RoadPark is a complete fun fair for FiveM. It turns the construction site on
the Mirror Park hillside into a park with paved paths, planting, festoon
lights, a fountain, a restroom pavilion and ambient visitors — and it
fills the park with six rideable attractions, seven fairground games and four
food stands.

Every attraction is its own resource. The map resource `roadpark` knows the
park slots and hands each attraction its position when that attraction starts.
Stop a resource and its slot simply stays empty.

## 🎢 Rides

| Ride | Resource | Seats | Default price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Breakdance | `roadpark_breakdance` | 32 (16 two-seat gondolas) | 2 chips |
| Highlander drop tower | `roadpark_highlander` | 24 | 2 chips |
| Grand Prix bumper cars | `roadpark_autoscooter` | 20 (10 cars, driver and passenger) | 1 chip per rider |
| Ferris Wheel | `roadpark_riesenrad` | 48 (12 capsules with 4 seats) | 1 chip |
| Klabautermann swing ship | `roadpark_swingboat` | 40 | 1 chip |
| Himmelsflieger wave swinger | `roadpark_wellenflug` | 48 (32 single and 8 double chairs) | 1 chip |

Rides take [Park Chips](/roadpark/tickets) while `roadpark_tickets` runs. Without
the ticket resource every ride is free.

## 🏛️ Park services

| Part | Resource | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Park map | `roadpark` | Ground, paths, planting, lights, restrooms, visitors, road closures and the ride slots |
| Ticket booth | `roadpark_tickets` | Sells Park Chips at the entrance |
| Entrance sign | `roadpark_entrance_sign` | Optional neon lettering on the gate with your own text |

## 📍 Where it is

The park sits on the hillside construction site along Mirror Park Boulevard. A
blip marks the entrance at **1306.0, -731.5**. The park covers about
13,200 m².

- **West gate:** entrance sign and ticket booth.
- **Central path:** the games row with Lucky Ducks, the fountain and the pergola.
- **North:** Breakdance, the Klabautermann swing ship and the Food Lane with all four food stands.
- **East:** the Ferris Wheel and the restroom pavilion by the terrace walk.
- **South:** the Himmelsflieger, the Claw Arcade, the Grand Prix bumper cars and the Highlander tower.

## 📋 Requirements

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="OneSync" icon="radio-tower">
    Required. Every RoadPark resource declares it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Framework" icon="layers">
    Optional. ESX, QBCore and Qbox are detected automatically. Standalone works too.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inventory" icon="backpack">
    Needed for chips and prizes. ox_inventory, qb-inventory and several others are detected.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No database" icon="database">
    No SQL import, no oxmysql and no ox_lib. Data lives in JSON files and server KVP.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  **Running without a framework.** The park, the rides and all games work
  standalone, but nothing can be paid for: rides, games and food are free, the
  ticket booth shows as closed and no item prizes are handed out.
</Info>
