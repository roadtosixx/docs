# Games

Press **E** at a slot machine, a table, the lucky wheel or a cashier. The
server gives you a free seat. **E** is a FiveM key binding (command
`/roadcasino`), so players can change it under **Settings → Key Bindings →
FiveM → Interact with RoadCasino**.

## 💵 Chips and cashiers

Chips are bought and sold at the cashier against the framework account set in
`Config.Account` (default `bank`).

| Cashier | Position |
|---|---|
| Main cashier, ground floor | (-459.1, -1012.9, 24.0) |
| VIP cashier, VIP floor | (-459.1, -1009.5, 30.0) |

- One exchange moves at most `Config.ExchangeMaximum` (default 1,000,000).
- Buying chips stops at `Config.MaximumBalance` (default 1,000,000,000).
  Winnings are not capped.
- [Cashback](/roadcasino/staff) is collected at the cashier.

Chips are stored per character in the database and survive a restart.

## 🎰 Slot machines

36 machines with six slot games, played on the machine's own screen:

- Candy Cascade
- Harbour Catch
- Classic Sevens
- Thunder Crown
- Tome of Fortune
- Dino Raiders

Stakes run from 1 to 500 chips per spin. Every machine has a **turbo mode**
and **autoplay** for 10, 25 or 50 spins. Free-spin features are landed in
play; on every game except Classic Sevens they can also be bought for 100
times the stake.

Up to `Config.ScreenPoolSize` machine screens (default 8) are drawn at once,
within `Config.ScreenDistance` metres.

## 🃏 Table games

| Game | Tables on the floor | Stakes on the floor | Rules |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Blackjack | 6 | 10–500 | Splits, doubles, insurance and side bets |
| Roulette | 4 | 10–500 | European single zero |
| Texas hold'em | 4 | Buy-in 200–500 | Multiplayer, side pots, no rake |
| Three card poker | 1 | 10–5,000 | Ante, Play and Pair Plus |

Baccarat (punto banco) is played in two of the VIP salons below.

### Roulette

Chips are laid on the 3D table. Players call the announced bets Voisins du
Zéro, Tiers du Cylindre, Orphelins and Jeu Zéro, bet on a number and its
neighbours, and double or repeat their last bets. The ball camera follows
every spin (`Config.RouletteBallCamera`).

Sitting down starts in the top view or at the seat
(`Config.RouletteDefaultView`).

### Texas hold'em

Players play against each other with real stacks. The buy-in is at least 20
times the table minimum and at most the table maximum. A stack that leaves
the table, also on a disconnect or a character switch, returns to the
player's chips.

### Decision time

A player has `Config.ActionSeconds` (default 30) for each decision at a table.

### Keyboard

Every table game can be played with the keyboard. While the table menu is
hidden, these keys act on the table:

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrow keys | Move, choose a bet or a stake |
| **Enter** | Confirm |
| **Space** | Main action |
| **Backspace** | Back |
| **H** / **S** / **D** / **X** | Hit / stand / double / split |
| **F** / **C** / **R** / **A** | Fold / call / raise / all in |

Every key can be rebound under **Settings → Key Bindings → FiveM → Casino
table: …**.

## 🎡 Lucky wheel

The lucky wheel stands in the lobby. Every character gets one free spin every
24 hours (`Config.LuckyWheelCooldownSeconds`).

## 👑 VIP salons

Eight private salons on the VIP floor, one table each:

| Salon | Game | Stakes |
|---|---|---|
| Emerald | Blackjack | 500–25,000 |
| Onyx | Baccarat | 1,000–50,000 |
| Bordeaux | Roulette | 250–25,000 |
| Panorama | Baccarat | 5,000–250,000 |
| Havana | Texas hold'em | Buy-in 2,000–100,000 |
| Saphir | Roulette | 1,000–50,000 |
| Jade | Three card poker | 500–25,000 |
| Royal | Blackjack | 2,500–100,000 |

To sit down, a player needs at least the table minimum in chips (at hold'em,
the minimum buy-in). The screen beside each salon door shows the game, the
minimum stake and the free seats. It reads **locked** while the player's chips
are below the minimum and **full** when every seat is taken.

### Drinks

Players in a salon order drinks from `Config.Drinks` and pay in chips:

| Drink | Price |
|---|---:|
| Champagne | 500 |
| Whisky | 300 |
| Martini | 350 |
| Red wine | 250 |
| Mojito | 300 |
| Espresso | 50 |

One order every `Config.DrinkCooldownSeconds` (default 20) per player. A
served glass stays for `Config.DrinkSeconds` (default 180).

## 🛗 Lift

The guest lift connects the lobby, the VIP floor and the roof terrace.

| Where | Key | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Call panel | **E** | Call the lift |
| Call panel on the VIP floor | **E** / **G** | Call it upwards / downwards |
| Inside | **Arrow up / down** | Choose the floor |
| Inside | **E** | Confirm the floor |
| Inside | **X** | Open the doors |

If the lift reports a fault, run
[`casino_lift_recover`](/roadcasino/commands) in the server console: the cabin
drives to its nearest landing.

## 🔎 Status

`/roadcasino_status` prints the state of the stations near you to your F8
console. It helps with a report about a missing table or machine.
