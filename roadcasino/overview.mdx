# RoadCasino

RoadCasino is a complete luxury casino for FiveM. It stands on the former
construction site at about **(-480, -975, 24)**: a two-wing building with a
double-height lobby, a gaming floor, eight private salons on the VIP floor, a
restaurant, bars and two roof terraces, connected by a guest lift.

Inside are 36 slot machines with six slot games, 23 gaming tables and a lucky
wheel. Every result and payout is decided on the server. Wallets and running
rounds are stored in the database and survive a restart, and a disconnect
never forfeits an accepted bet.

## 📦 Resources

The download holds three resources. Keep their folder names unchanged.

| Resource | Content |
|---|---|
| `road_casino` | The script: games, cashier, lift, staff menu |
| `road_casino_map` | The building, its grounds and the road connection |
| `road_casino_props` | Furniture, game tables, slot cabinets and lift parts |

`road_world_map`, a separate download, holds the original GTA map files that
several RoadShop maps change. See [Installation](/roadcasino/installation).

## 🎰 Games

| Game | Places | Stakes on the gaming floor |
|---|---:|---|
| Slot machines | 36 | 1–500 chips per spin |
| Blackjack | 8 tables | 10–500 |
| Roulette | 6 tables | 10–500 |
| Texas hold'em | 5 tables | Buy-in 200–500 |
| Baccarat | 2 tables | Salons only |
| Three card poker | 2 tables | 10–5,000 |
| Lucky wheel | 1 | One free spin per character every 24 hours |

Eight of the tables stand in the private salons with higher stakes. See
[Games](/roadcasino/games) for every game and the salon limits.

## 👑 VIP floor

Eight private salons with one table each: Emerald, Onyx, Bordeaux, Panorama,
Havana, Saphir, Jade and Royal. A screen beside every door shows the game, the
minimum stake and the free seats. Players in a salon order drinks to the table
and pay in chips.

## 📊 Running the house

Players with your casino job open the staff menu: they search players, read
their statistics and assign ranks. Management also sees the house dashboard,
sets cashback rates and can pause all games. See
[Staff & ranks](/roadcasino/staff).
