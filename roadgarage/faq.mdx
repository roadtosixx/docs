# FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My vehicles do not show up in the garage">
    - RoadGarage reads the vehicles from your framework's table
      (`owned_vehicles` on ESX, `player_vehicles` on QBCore and Qbox). A
      vehicle must be owned by the character you play.
    - A garage only lists vehicles of its kind: boats in boat garages,
      aircraft in hangars and helipads.
    - Vehicles that are out or impounded appear under **Away** or
      **Impounded**, not under **In this garage**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="&quot;All parking spots are blocked.&quot;">
    Every parking slot of the garage has another vehicle within
    `Config.SpawnClearance` metres. Move the vehicles away, or add more slots
    to the garage's `spawns`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Players get no keys">
    RoadGarage detects the common key scripts on its own (qb-vehiclekeys,
    qbx_vehiclekeys, qs-vehiclekeys, wasabi_carlock and others). For any other
    script, give the keys in `ClientHooks.GiveKeys` in `client/hooks.lua` and
    return `true`. See [Hooks](/roadgarage/configuration#hooks).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fuel is not saved">
    The fuel scripts listed under [Hooks](/roadgarage/configuration#hooks) are
    detected on their own. For another script, read and set the level in
    `ClientHooks.GetFuel` and `ClientHooks.SetFuel`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="E opens another script, or vehicles appear twice">
    Another garage script is still running. Two garage systems compete for the
    same vehicles and the same key. Stop the old one and keep only RoadGarage.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Database errors about missing columns">
    Your database user may not change tables. Run `sql/install.sql` (and
    `sql/esx_columns.sql` on ESX) yourself, or `sql/migrations/1.1.0.sql` when
    you update from 1.0.0. See [Installation](/roadgarage/installation).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A vehicle shows as Missing">
    It is out, but no longer exists in the world, for example after a server
    restart. The owner recovers it at any garage or impound lot for
    `Config.RecoveryFee`. With `Config.ReturnLostOnStart = true`, missing
    vehicles return to their last garage when RoadGarage starts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it work with RoadPhone's Garage app?">
    Yes. RoadGarage keeps vehicle state in the framework's own columns, so the
    app and the valet keep working. With `Config.roadGarage = true` in
    RoadPhone 3.0, the app also shows garage names, nicknames, mileage,
    impound details and shared vehicles. See
    [Installation](/roadgarage/installation#roadphone).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use it without a framework?">
    Yes. RoadGarage runs on ESX Legacy, QBCore, Qbox and standalone. OneSync,
    oxmysql (or mysql-async) and ox_lib are always required.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The Discord logs stay empty">
    Webhook URLs go into `API.lua`, not `config.lua`. A channel without a URL
    uses `default`; when `default` is empty too, nothing is posted. See
    [Discord logs](/roadgarage/configuration#discord-logs).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add my own garage?">
    Add an entry to `Config.Garages` with coordinates for the attendant and at
    least one parking slot. See
    [Adding a garage](/roadgarage/configuration#adding-a-garage). Housing and
    other scripts can add garages at runtime with
    [`RegisterGarage`](/roadgarage/api/exports#registergarage).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
