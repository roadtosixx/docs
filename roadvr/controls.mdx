# Controls

## Two modes

RoadVR always runs in one of two modes, and knowing which one you are in
explains almost every question about the controls.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Passthrough" icon="footprints">
    Windows float around you. You walk, drive and play normally — the windows
    are pure graphics and take no input.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Focus" icon="mouse-pointer-click">
    The mouse is free and you can click. Movement is locked while you are in
    it — except while you hold a window's grabber, which lets you walk and
    carry the window along.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You enter focus mode by looking at a window and pressing `E`, and you leave it
with `ESC`. Nothing is torn down on the way out — the windows stay exactly
where they were.

<Info>
  The headset only goes on in **first person**. Switching to third person while
  it is on takes it off again — flying the drone is the exception. Servers can
  turn this off with `Config.FirstPersonOnly = false`.
</Info>

## The keys

| Input | What it does | Default | Changed through |
|---|---|---|---|
| Use the item | Put the headset on and take it off | — | `Config.Item` |
| `roadvr_toggle` | The same without an item | unbound | FiveM key bindings |
| `F7` | Home screen | `F7` | FiveM key bindings |
| `F6` | Control Centre | `F6` | `Config.ControlCenter.key`, FiveM key bindings |
| `roadvr_widgets` | Widget editor | unbound | `Config.Widgets.editKey`, FiveM key bindings |
| `E` while looking at a window | Focus it, free the mouse | `E` | `Config.FocusKey` |
| `ESC` | Leave focus, back to passthrough | — | — |

<Note>
  `F7`, `F6`, `roadvr_toggle` and `roadvr_widgets` are **FiveM keybinds**: each
  player can rebind them under Settings → Key Bindings → FiveM, and what
  `config.lua` sets is only the default. The focus key is not — it reads a
  fixed game control, so `Config.FocusKey` accepts `E`, `G`, `X`, `F` or `H`
  and falls back to `E` for anything else.
</Note>

`F7` does a little more than open the home screen: in a car with the cockpit
bar enabled it shows and hides the bar in the windshield, and if the home
screen is already open but far behind you, it brings it back in front.

`ESC` also closes the widget editor, and while an environment is running it
opens a small menu in passthrough: **Resume**, **Open map** and **Leave
environment**.

## The unbound keys

`roadvr_toggle` and `roadvr_widgets` appear in the FiveM key bindings and are
deliberately shipped **without a default key**.

That is not an oversight. A default would take a key away from every server
that installs RoadVR. Most servers hand the headset out as an item anyway, and
the widget editor has its own button on the home screen. If you run without an
item (`Config.Item = ''`), tell your players to bind `roadvr_toggle` once
themselves — or use the command:

```
/roadvr_toggle
```

<Tip>
  When no window is near, a hint at the bottom of the view reads *"Press F7 to
  open the home screen"* — with whatever key the player actually bound. Nobody
  is left wearing a headset that shows nothing.
</Tip>

## The window bar

Every window has a bar of controls hanging **below** it. It appears when you
look at the window, and from left to right it holds:

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| App buttons | Whatever the app puts there |
| ✕ | Close the window |
| Grabber | Drag to move the window. Hold it and walk to carry the window along. |
| Tilt | Drag up or down to tilt the window up to 60°. Double-click sets it straight again. |
| Anchor | Pin the window in the room, or let it come along with you |
| Arrange | Line up every open window side by side in front of you and pin them |
| Move all | The grabber, the wheel and the tilt of any window act on all of them |
| Home | Bring the home screen to where this window is |

The home screen's own bar carries a **Widgets** button in place of Home.

## Moving windows

A window hangs on a sphere around the player rather than in free 3D space, so
dragging the grabber moves it along that shell.

- **Distance:** the mouse wheel over the grabber pulls the window closer or
  pushes it away, in 0.2 m steps between 0.8 m and 6 m.
- **Size:** move the pointer near a corner and an arc appears — drag it. The
  window keeps its proportions.
- **Facing:** with **Windows turn with you** on (the default, in Settings →
  Windows) a window always faces you. Switched off, it keeps the direction it
  was placed in.

<Tip>
  While **Move all** is on, every grab handle is tinted, because an invisible
  mode that changes what dragging does is a trap.
</Tip>

## Anchors

Two anchor modes decide what happens when you walk away:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Come along" icon="person-standing">
    The window follows you once you are 2 m away or have turned 45°. Good for
    anything you want to keep reading while you move.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stay in place" icon="map-pin">
    The window stays where you put it. Good for leaving a screen on a desk.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

New windows **stay in place** by default. Each player changes that under
Settings → Windows → **New windows**, and every window can be switched on its
own with the anchor button in its bar. A window you drag and let go of is
pinned in the room where you left it.

## How many windows

`Config.MaxPanels` (4) caps the app windows that can be open at once. The home
screen, Spaces and the car's displays do not count. Opening one more closes the
oldest, with a notification saying so.

## Widgets

Open the widget editor with the **Widgets** button under the home screen, the
**Widgets** switch in the Control Centre, or `/roadvr_widgets`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a widget">
    The bar shows how many you have up out of your limit. Widgets with content
    — a note, a photo, a sign — open a sheet first; **Hang it up** when it is
    ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place it">
    The widget follows your gaze. The mouse wheel resizes it, **left click**
    sticks it to the wall, **right click** cancels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change it later">
    In the editor, click a widget on the wall to take it off, use the pencil to
    change its contents, choose another widget to swap it in the same spot, or
    remove it with ✕. **Done** or `ESC` closes the editor.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Widgets live in the player's profile, so they survive taking the headset off,
relogging and a resource restart.

## Learn it in game

**Window basics** is a short interactive tutorial that walks through focusing,
grabbing, distance, tilt, resizing, anchors, Move all and Arrange. It is
offered at the end of the first setup and can be started again from Settings →
General → **Try the window tutorial**. It puts your open windows away while it
runs and brings them back afterwards.
